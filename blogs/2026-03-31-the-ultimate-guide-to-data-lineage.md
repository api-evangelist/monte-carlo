When a number in an executive dashboard looks wrong, most organizations face the same problem: nobody can say with certainty where that number came from, what calculations it passed through, or which team owns the fix. Data lineage makes these questions answerable. It tracks where data originates, how it transforms as it moves through systems, and where it ultimately surfaces for the people and processes that depend on it. For data engineers, that means faster debugging and safer deployments....
